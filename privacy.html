PRIVACY POLICY

Your scans stay yours.

Updated: November 9, 2026

Account-free QR Scanner 1.0: The initial public release is designed for scanning, creating, saving and exporting QR codes and barcodes directly on your iPhone or iPad. There is no QR Scanner sign-in, cloud backup, hosted dynamic QR service, or advertising SDK. This policy also explains older pre-release test versions below.

Information processed locally

  • Camera frames and photos you explicitly select to scan codes, processed on your device.
  • QR and barcode values, saved scan history, favorites, collections, notes and labels, stored on your device.
  • Content entered to create ordinary QR codes, such as website links, Wi-Fi credentials, text and contact information.
  • Color, appearance, sound and other app preferences.

The account-free release does not transmit this information to a QR Scanner developer-operated server or require an account. It does not automatically read your clipboard or automatically upload your history. Ordinary static QR codes encode their destination directly; they do not require our hosting service and are not expired by an Apple subscription ending.

When you choose another service

If you specifically tap Look up product for a retail barcode, your device sends that selected barcode to Open Food Facts through an HTTPS request. As with other website and API requests, the provider can see the connection IP address and standard request information. Open Food Facts publishes a privacy policy stating that visitor-IP logs may be retained for up to three years for security, technical analysis and popularity measurement. We do not control the provider's logging or retention practices. This optional lookup does not send your private scan history to the QR Scanner developer, and no product lookup occurs merely because you scan a barcode.

You may separately choose to open GS1 verification in your browser; that action copies the selected barcode to your clipboard for you to paste, but does not automatically submit it to GS1. External websites and services apply their own privacy policies. Opening websites, sharing a QR code, placing calls, saving to Photos and adding contacts or events occur only after your chosen action and may involve Apple or the destination provider.

Apple purchases

Optional premium features are available as Apple in-app purchases. Apple manages transactions, subscriptions, refunds and the verification information delivered through StoreKit. The account-free release does not use a third-party billing SDK or send purchases to a QR Scanner account server. Manage or restore purchases through the in-app Settings or your Apple Account.

Retention and deletion

Your scan history stays on your device. Deleted history items may remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted. You can clear history and permanently remove records using the app's own History controls. Content you explicitly export or save to Photos is controlled by you and the chosen destination.

Advertising and tracking

The account-free release includes no cross-app advertising or tracking SDKs and does not request Apple's tracking permission. We do not sell your private scan values, notes or saved history.

Earlier pre-release / TestFlight versions

Some internal pre-release builds offered opt-in Clerk sign-in, encrypted Supabase backup, and hosted dynamic QR redirects. If you used those features in an older test build, information you explicitly supplied to them may remain in those providers' systems and is not automatically deleted by updating to an account-free release. The earlier version's account identifier, optional sign-in email, encrypted backup records and hosted redirect destinations/aggregate visit data are subject to the disclosures provided when those services were available. The original optional encryption passphrase was not uploaded to the service.

We have removed these online features from the initial public app, but have not automatically deleted earlier test accounts, hosted destinations or cloud backups. If you previously used those services and need help with retained cloud data, use the support page to request an appropriate private handling path. Please never put passwords, personal account identifiers, private QR payloads or other sensitive information in a public support issue.
