PRIVACY POLICY

Your scans stay yours.

Effective date: September 26, 2026

QR & Barcode Scanner is designed to perform its core scanning, code creation, and history functions on your iPhone.

Information processed on device

  • Camera frames used to recognize QR codes and barcodes.
  • Photos you explicitly select for code recognition.
  • Decoded QR/barcode values and scan history.
  • Content you enter when creating a QR code.
  • App preferences stored by iOS.

These items are not uploaded to the app developer as part of the core scanning experience. Scan history is stored locally on your device.

Actions you choose

When you choose an action such as opening a website, searching for a barcode, opening Maps, placing a call, composing a message or email, adding a contact or calendar event, saving an image, or sharing content, iOS or the selected third-party service receives the information necessary to perform that action. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes purchase and account information needed to complete and restore purchases. The app also contains optional support for RevenueCat as a purchase-entitlement service. When RevenueCat is configured in a release, it may process purchase, product, device, and anonymous customer-identification information needed to manage entitlements. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not include advertising and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission for cross-app tracking. The app does not sell scanned content or personal information.

Deletion and retention

You can delete individual history entries or clear scan history in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. App Store purchase records remain subject to Apple's retention practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, use the official support page. Do not include private QR contents, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, or other sensitive scan data in a public support request.
