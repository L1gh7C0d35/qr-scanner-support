PRIVACY POLICY

Your scans stay yours.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

QR & Barcode Scanner keeps basic camera scanning, static QR creation, and scan history on your device by default. The optional account, encrypted backup, and dynamic QR features are separate. You can scan codes without creating an account.

Information processed on your device

  • Camera frames and photos you select to recognize QR codes and barcodes.
  • Decoded values, saved history, labels, notes, favorites and collections.
  • Information you enter to create static QR codes, including optional Wi-Fi or contact data.
  • Appearance, haptics and other app preferences.

For these core features, the app does not transmit camera frames or private scan history to its servers. Scans and creations can contain sensitive information, so only share them intentionally.

Optional account and login

If you choose to sign in for account-based features, our authentication provider, Clerk, processes sign-in credentials, your email address (when used for login), and an account identifier. We use that identifier to ensure only your account can manage your hosted QR codes or encrypted backups. No account is needed for standard scanning.

Optional encrypted account sync

When you explicitly enable encrypted sync, your scan history, collections and personal notes are encrypted on your device using a passphrase you create. Supabase stores the encrypted records and their account ID, record IDs, modification dates, and a random encryption salt. We do not transmit or retain your passphrase and cannot recover it. If you lose that passphrase, an existing encrypted backup may be inaccessible.

Cloud backup is off by default. You can disconnect an individual device or permanently delete the encrypted cloud backup from Sync Settings. Disconnecting alone does not delete a saved backup. Deleted scans can remain in local Recently Deleted for up to 30 days, except when you choose permanent deletion.

Optional dynamic QR links

If you create hosted dynamic QR links, Supabase stores your account ID, a link name, destination address, enabled state, and aggregate visit counts. The printed code opens our hosted redirect address rather than a direct static destination. Unlike a static QR code, a hosted code needs the redirect service to remain available. Deleting or disabling a hosted link can make printed codes stop redirecting.

To protect the service from abuse, redirect requests use a short-lived cryptographic digest of the request address for rate limiting. The application database does not save visitors' raw IP addresses, names, user agents or individual location histories. Temporary rate-limit records are scheduled for deletion after 48 hours; daily aggregate visit counts are scheduled for deletion after 90 days. All-time counts remain until you delete the link. Infrastructure providers may process ordinary request logs under their own policies.

Product lookups and other choices

If you explicitly request an external product lookup, only the selected barcode is sent to the chosen provider, such as Open Food Facts. You can also open GS1 verification in your browser. Those services have their own privacy terms. Opening links, copying content, calling, composing messages, and exporting files are user-initiated actions managed by iOS or the selected service. We do not silently run barcode product searches.

Purchases and service providers

Apple processes App Store payments and purchase entitlements. The app also includes optional purchase integration with RevenueCat; if enabled, it may process the information necessary to confirm your purchases. Clerk provides sign-in, and Supabase provides the optional sync and hosted QR service. We use these services for app functionality, not for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not show third-party advertising or request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission for cross-app tracking. We do not sell private scan contents or personal information.

Retention and deletion

On-device History can be deleted through the app; Recently Deleted items are retained for up to 30 days unless deleted permanently. You can delete optional encrypted cloud backups separately and remove hosted dynamic links, including their aggregate scan statistics. Account and payment information held by providers may be subject to their legal retention obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit the support page. Do not include passwords, private QR contents, contact details, or other sensitive data in public requests.
