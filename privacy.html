PRIVACY POLICY

Your scans stay yours.

Updated: November 16, 2026

Which app version do you have? QR & Barcode Scanner is an account-free, local-first app. The free-only 1.0 (Build 29) has no subscription, trial, in-app purchase or Restore screen. Newer paid-capable TestFlight builds (Build 30 and later, when available) retain free scanning, essential QR and barcode creation, and your complete local history, while offering optional QR Scanner Pro designs, additional QR types and professional export tools through Apple StoreKit. There is no QR Scanner sign-in, cloud backup, hosted dynamic QR service, developer-operated advertising or barcode product lookup API request in these current local-first builds. Your installed version determines which purchase controls appear. Older pre-release online features are explained below.

Information processed locally

  • Camera frames and photos you explicitly select to scan codes, processed on your device.
  • QR and barcode values, saved scan history, favorites, collections, notes and labels, stored on your device.
  • Content entered to create ordinary QR codes, such as website links, Wi-Fi credentials, text and contact information.
  • Color, appearance, sound and other app preferences.

These current local-first builds do not transmit scans, saved history, QR designs, selected photos or entered content to a QR Scanner developer-operated server or require an account. The app does not automatically read your clipboard or upload your history. Ordinary static QR codes encode their destination directly; they do not require our hosting service and are not expired by an Apple subscription ending.

When you choose another service

Build 29 and the current StoreKit-enabled local-first TestFlight builds do not contact a product-lookup provider when you scan a barcode. Some earlier TestFlight builds included a separate Look up product action for retail barcodes: only when selected, that action sent the selected barcode in an HTTPS request to Open Food Facts. That provider could see the connecting IP address and standard request information and says its visitor-IP logs may be retained for up to three years. We cannot confirm its exact API-path/IP linkage or retention. Those old optional lookups did not send your saved scan history to a QR Scanner developer server; they are disabled in current local-first builds.

You may separately choose to open GS1 verification in your browser; that action copies the selected barcode to your clipboard for you to paste, but does not automatically submit it to GS1. External websites and services apply their own privacy policies. Opening websites, sharing a QR code, placing calls, saving to Photos and adding contacts or events occur only after your chosen action and may involve Apple or the destination provider.

Optional Pro purchases and older TestFlight builds

The free-only Build 29 has no in-app purchase, subscription, trial or Restore screen. In paid-capable builds, QR Scanner Pro offers optional Apple-managed weekly, monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Available plans, your local App Store price, renewal terms and any introductory-trial eligibility are displayed in the app by Apple StoreKit before checkout. Scanning, ordinary QR and barcode creation, and your complete saved history remain free regardless of whether you buy Pro. Apple processes payment information, purchase authorization, renewals, refunds and subscription management. The app reads Apple-verified purchase entitlements to enable Pro, and offers Restore Purchases where StoreKit purchases are available. We do not operate a separate payment server, collect payment-card information or transmit saved scans, QR contents or history through this purchase process. For purchases made in older TestFlight builds, review your Apple Account purchase history or contact Support.

Retention and deletion

Your scan history stays on your device. Deleted history items may remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted. You can clear history and permanently remove records using the app's own History controls. Content you explicitly export or save to Photos is controlled by you and the chosen destination.

Advertising and tracking

The current local-first free-only and paid-capable builds include no cross-app advertising or tracking SDKs and do not request Apple's tracking permission. We do not sell your private scan values, notes or saved history.

Earlier pre-release / TestFlight versions

Some internal pre-release builds offered opt-in Clerk sign-in, encrypted Supabase backup, and hosted dynamic QR redirects. If you used those features in an older test build, information you explicitly supplied to them may remain in those providers' systems and is not automatically deleted by updating to an account-free release. The earlier version's account identifier, optional sign-in email, encrypted backup records and hosted redirect destinations/aggregate visit data are subject to the disclosures provided when those services were available. The original optional encryption passphrase was not uploaded to the service.

These online features are not part of the current free-only or paid-capable local-first builds, but earlier test accounts, hosted destinations or cloud backups have not necessarily been deleted. If you previously used those services and need help with retained cloud data, use the support page to ask for a private assistance channel, without including any identifying details in the initial public request. A secure verification/contact method must be established before account or cloud records can be reviewed or deleted. Please never put passwords, email addresses, account identifiers, private QR payloads or other sensitive information in a public support issue.
