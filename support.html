How can we help?

Before reporting a scan issue

  • Give Camera access in iPhone Settings if live scanning is unavailable.
  • For a saved screenshot or photo, use the photo scan action instead of the live camera.
  • For damaged or very small codes, improve lighting and move the camera until the code is sharp.
  • Premium purchases can be recovered with Restore Purchases in the app.

Report a problem

Open a support request on GitHub.

Please include your iOS version, app version, and a description of what happened. Never post Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, private QR payloads, purchase receipts, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
