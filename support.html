How can we help?

Before reporting a scan issue

  • Give Camera access in iPhone Settings if live scanning is unavailable.
  • For a saved screenshot or photo, use the photo scan action instead of the live camera.
  • For damaged or very small codes, improve lighting and move the camera until the code is sharp.

Pro purchases, free trials and Restore

The free-only 1.0 Build 29 has no purchase, trial or Restore screen. Newer paid-capable builds offer optional QR Scanner Pro through Apple's in-app purchase system. Available plans include weekly, monthly and annual subscriptions or a one-time lifetime purchase. The app displays your actual App Store price, any trial you are eligible for, and the renewal terms before you confirm a purchase.

  • Restore a previous purchase: If your build has Pro, open Settings → Restore purchases or select Restore Purchases on the Pro offer screen. Restore can check existing Apple purchases; it does not create a new subscription. If no active entitlement is found, Pro stays locked.
  • Cancel or manage a subscription: Use Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account, also linked from the app's Settings. Weekly, monthly and annual plans renew until canceled. Cancel before your next billing date if you do not want to renew. QR Scanner cannot cancel an Apple subscription on your behalf.
  • Request a refund: Use Apple's Report a Problem service, also linked from the app's Settings. Apple handles the purchase, refund decision and subscription status; there is no separate QR Scanner payment account.
  • Keep using the free app: Scanning, essential QR and barcode creation, and your complete locally saved History remain available without Pro, including after a subscription expires. The one-time lifetime purchase does not auto-renew.

A free trial is offered only when Apple confirms your account is eligible. Purchases made in TestFlight use Apple's Sandbox and do not incur ordinary App Store charges. TestFlight purchase testing does not mean a paid public version has launched.

Report a problem

Open a support request on GitHub.

Please include your iOS version, app version, and a description of what happened. Never post Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, private QR payloads, purchase receipts, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
